What we do What is Goodoo?

Goodoo is a Bengaluru not-for-profit giving community animals, animals rescued from illegal breeders, and abandoned geriatric animals the care and attention they need.

Goodoo Animal Welfare Trust is a not-for-profit established to work towards a better standard of living for community animals, domestic animals confiscated from illegal breeders, and geriatric animals who are abandoned or given up because they require more care and attention.

Our centre

A cage-free home built for rescue and recovery.

Goodoo Rescue and Rehab Centre is built on an 80,000 sq ft mango orchard. At capacity, we will be able to house over 150 rescued dogs. Goodoo is an entirely cage-free facility, and our rescues have the run of the space.

Our team

Goodoo Managing Committee

Anoopa Anand

CEO and Executive Director

Goodoo is run and managed by Anoopa Anand, a seasoned animal welfare worker with over 14 years of experience in the sector. She is solely responsible for running the day-to-day operations of the trust and putting in place the processes and protocols at Goodoo Rescue and Rehab Centre and Serene's Home for Senior Dogs. Anoopa’s focus will be to push urban animals welfare into a professional, well-organised sector, keeping high-end caregiving and a practical approach at the heart of Goodoo's work.

Goodoo Board of Trustees

Devika Rao

Market Research & Communications · Ex Accenture and Puma

Devika brings market research and brand communications experience from Accenture and Puma to a trust that has to earn public attention, not demand it. She helps Goodoo speak in a voice that is clear, specific, and easy for Bengaluru to trust.

On the board she watches how rescue stories, donor updates, and neighbourhood campaigns land — so the work on the street is matched by comms that stay honest and useful.

Smriti Rana

Head, Strategic Programmes & Partnerships · Pallium India

Smriti leads strategic programmes and partnerships at Pallium India, and brings that same discipline of care-systems to Goodoo’s board. She knows how a small team stays effective when the need on the ground never pauses.

As a trustee she helps the organisation build partnerships that last, and programmes that can grow without losing the quality of care each animal receives.

Dr. Shilpaa Anand

Humanities Department · BITS Pilani, Hyderabad

Shilpaa teaches in the Humanities Department at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad. Her academic work sits with questions of care, disability, and how cultures decide whose lives are worth attending to.

On the board she keeps Goodoo’s ethics and language honest — how we talk about street animals, caregivers, and the city they share — so the trust’s story never outruns the work.

Chitra Raghavan

Partner · Rajaram Legal

Chitra is a partner at Rajaram Legal and brings legal clarity to a trust that handles rescues, land, donations, and public accountability every week. She helps keep the paperwork as sturdy as the kennels.

As a trustee she watches governance, compliance, and the quiet decisions that protect the animals — and the people who show up for them — when something goes wrong.

Swaroop Bhartiya

Sr. Marketing and Content Professional

Swaroop is a senior marketing and content professional who helps Goodoo find a public voice that is warm and specific — never guilt-heavy, never vague. He treats every campaign as a chance to make one more person care enough to act.

On the board he guides how stories, updates, and outreach stay true to the animals and to the people doing the daily work of feeding, treating, and holding space.

Shilpa Colluru

Head—Awareness and Outreach

Shilpa heads Awareness and Outreach — the work of getting Bengaluru to see street animals as neighbours, not noise. She connects volunteers, apartment groups, and the trust so a rescue does not stay invisible.

As a trustee she keeps Goodoo present in the city: more people who know who to call, how to help, and why a well-run rehab centre matters for the dogs who live on our streets.
